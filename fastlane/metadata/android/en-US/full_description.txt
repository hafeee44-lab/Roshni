<p>Roshni watches your hybrid solar inverter and tells you the moment the mains go off: a full-screen alarm over the lock screen, with how long your battery will last.</p>

<p><b>Works with your inverter</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Directly over your home Wi-Fi</b>, with no account or API key: Deye, Sunsynk and Sol-Ark, plus (beta) Sofar, Solis, KSTAR, Afore, Megarevo, INVT and other inverters with a Solarman Wi-Fi logger</li>
<li><b>Through your inverter's cloud</b>: Solarman, plus (beta) Growatt, Huawei FusionSolar, SolisCloud and DESS Monitor (SmartESS / WatchPower, used by many Voltronic-based inverters)</li>
</ul>
<p>Beta brands are built from their published specifications and still being confirmed on real hardware. Reports from owners are very welcome.</p>

<p><b>Alerts that reach you</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Full-screen outage alarm with slide to stop; choose how long it rings</li>
<li>Optional ring in silent mode, quiet hours, and a "mains back on" notice</li>
<li>Low and critical battery, heavy load, inverter faults and overheating</li>
<li>Optional WhatsApp relay so the whole household hears</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Numbers you can trust</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Careful detection: grid relay, frequency, voltage and power balance, confirmed before anything rings</li>
<li>Battery runtime that learns your real capacity, and how long it lasts if you cut back to your usual low use</li>
<li>Never shows stale data as current</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Your own history</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Imports the last two weeks from Solarman on first use</li>
<li>Learns your load-shedding schedule and warns before the next cut</li>
<li>Outage map, day charts, uptime and CSV export</li>
</ul>

<p>Home-screen widget, Quick Settings tile, English and Urdu, light and dark themes, and a demo mode to try everything without an inverter.</p>

<p>Free and open source. No ads, no analytics, no accounts of our own: the app only talks to your inverter's logger or your inverter's cloud and, if you set one up, your own WhatsApp bridge.</p>
